Our Mission

2 Hearts 4 Paws Refuge

"We rescue dogs who have been abandoned by their owners, chained or tethered, emergency situations, and occasionally we will take a dog from the local shelter and/or Animal Control. We provide food, water, shelter, and lots of love! We get them vaccinated and spayed/neutered. If they are adoptable, we will find them GOOD homes—and if they are not adoptable then they will stay with us the rest of their lives. We are a refuge."

Emergency Rescue

24/7 immediate response and first aid for abandoned, abused, or injured animals in the area.

Temporary Sanctuary

Providing a clean shelter, full nutrition, and professional medical care for every furry friend.

Forever Homes

Carefully screening and matching pets with loving families to ensure a happy future for all.
